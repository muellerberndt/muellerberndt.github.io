Observer Patch Holography

OPH asks a simple physics question: how can limited systems build one shared world from partial views? It starts with bounded systems that have a local state, a boundary, memory, a way to read themselves and nearby systems, and a way to repair disagreement. A shared fact has to be recorded, compared, and able to survive a correction.

OPH connects shared records to quantum probability, thermodynamics, spacetime geometry and gauge structure under explicit assumptions. Specified observer populations and field laws support controlled continuum comparisons. Calculations of constants carry their own physical assumptions and comparison status. The papers, Lean proofs and executable receipts make each step inspectable.

The Standard Model

The gauge Lie type from twelve ports

Under complete reversible response and internal transport, a classification theorem fixes the twelve-port response to the Standard Model gauge Lie type. A declared fifteen-state representation supplies an anomaly-free matter grammar. Identifying physical matter requires a separate argument.

Quantum observation

Quantum structure from public records

Under stated probability assumptions, finite event algebras support Born representation and Lüders conditioning. A specified free scalar action on prepared addresses has a joint spatial/time detector limit and a separated response larger than the certified error, without executing its large tensor history. A separate 64-site record history supports an inferred action clock and interval-wide quantum comparisons. Action, preparation and physical calibration retain explicit assumptions.

Thermodynamics and constants

Repair becomes a physical principle

A conditional finite theorem package recovers the four laws of thermodynamics from consensus repair with specified thermal assumptions. Koide's identity uses a stated balance premise; the fine-structure calculation is diagnostic. Exact interval bounds make these calculations concrete targets for derivation and falsification.

How The Idea Works

A shared world needs records that survive comparison

1 / Local view

Each patch sees only part

Each observer patch has a local state, a boundary, and limited access to the world around it. It keeps records of what it can read.

2 / Read and compare

Records can become public

A record becomes a shared fact when other patches can read it, compare it with their own records, and check the overlap.

3 / Repair

Disagreement triggers a check

Conflicting records call for an update, a repeated measurement, or a better explanation. The repair process preserves the evidence needed for others to check the result.

4 / Continuation

Records need to last

Continuation research examines how a record can retain its role as the system changes, so other observers can use and test it.
