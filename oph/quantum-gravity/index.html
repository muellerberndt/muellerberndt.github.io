Quantum Gravity.

OPH explores whether gravity can grow out of the way limited observers compare shared evidence. Each observer sees only part of a world, keeps records, and has to resolve conflicts with its neighbours.

Under stated assumptions, the mathematics connects shared records and quantum information to geometry and gravitational dynamics. A physical link to our universe requires a separate test.

One local mathematical closure has a checked solution. A separate proposal asks whether a universe could read its own total record capacity. That proposal supplies no cosmological value without the general proof and the physical identification. Several numerical comparisons are retrospective and have no predictive status.

How OPH reaches gravity

OPH begins with bounded patches that can compare records where they overlap. The programme asks whether the rules needed for reliable agreement can also organise geometry and gravity.

Authenticated read-from records generate a finite, causal-set-like poset. Lean proves that source height is exactly the attained maximum authenticated-parent-chain length. It also proves that every finite decidable strict partial order compiles exactly into the authenticated provenance grammar; the order to be compiled is supplied, so this is an expressivity result rather than OPH selection. Independently of event height, the exact rank-three source Gram quotient defines the four-dimensional ambient target carrier R ⊕ Vsrc with Lorentz signature (+---); every source-unit spatial direction u determines the future-null vector (1,u).

Every finite semantic log has an explicit auxiliary injective placement in that 1+3 carrier which sends generated ancestry into the future cone and makes distinct equal-height events spacelike. It uses an arbitrary enumeration along one source axis, so it is neither source-selected nor order-reflecting. For a source-selected placement, the parent-edge speed bound, equal-height spatial injectivity, and strict spacelike separation of increasing-height non-ancestors imply exact cone/order agreement; event separation and exact source-interval preservation follow. In standard causal-set terminology this is an exact finite order-embedding criterion; a faithful embedding additionally requires approximately Poisson density. Physical signal-cone agreement, count-to-volume calibration, a source-selected refinement family, manifoldlikeness, and stable dimension and topology are necessary for effective smooth spacetime.

Exact simulator controls sharpen the boundary. All 30 authenticated Hasse links in the frozen 24-event log receive local port labels and normalized future-null representatives in their hidden rank-three carrier templates. The used directions have per-carrier ranks 2, 1, and 2. Thus no carrier spans rank three and no frame gluing or global placement is obtained. Separately, four-seed Poisson-sprinkled Minkowski 3+1 controls at target means 64, 128, and 256 compile without order distortion; every coordinate map is injective and every point remains inside the sampled interval. Their mean Myrheim–Meyer estimates are 4.02824, 3.84595, and 3.90346. Geometry, density, population, read pattern, and placement are imported; only carriers and transitive orders are nested, not canonical source logs. This is source-grammar compatibility and supplies no OPH selection, similarity, manifoldlikeness, or continuum. The native 2,304-event diagnostic is dissimilar to its frozen exploratory 3+1 band at this cutoff. The repository publishes the source-history evidence and causet-likeness evidence with their independent checkers.

On the same finite event type, in a separately supplied 3+1 tensor interface, a minimal theorem turns nine supplied balances along exact source-unit tomography directions, Ward/Bianchi conservation, and connectedness into all-null balance and an Einstein-form identity. It does not identify tensor-coordinate differences with differences in the constructed carrier. The poset does not construct the tensor fields, step, or balance laws. Smooth Einstein promotion needs physical stress-energy, coupling, vacuum, and scale plus either same-family tensor-curvature convergence or the independent continuum small-ball/null-balance identification; scalar-curvature convergence alone is diagnostic.
