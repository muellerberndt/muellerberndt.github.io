Introduction and claim boundary

Physical theory ordinarily postulates its deepest structures separately. Quantum theory posits a Hilbert space and the Born rule; relativity posits a Lorentzian manifold; the Standard Model posits a gauge group and matter representations. Reconstruction programs ask whether some of these postulates are theorems of something smaller. This paper presents one such program, Observer Patch Holography (OPH). Its primitive is a finite society of observers that keep records, compare them where they overlap, and repair disagreement.

The central question is operational:

Can operational consistency among finite observers reconstruct the mathematical structures ordinarily postulated separately in quantum theory, spacetime geometry, and gauge physics?

Our answer is a structured partial yes, and the structure is the point. Some reconstructions are finite theorems with machine-checked proofs. Some are exact computations with executable certificates. Some are conditional compositions whose physical antecedents are stated as named premises. Some require physical producers that are open obligations. This paper never mixes those classes. Every result below carries one of four labels:

T proved finite theorem (formal or exact-arithmetic proof)
W machine-certified finite witness (executable certificate, controls)
C conditional reconstruction theorem (exact, with named premises)
O open physical producer (required attachment, no construction on record)

The discipline matters because reconstruction programs fail most often by silently promoting an interpretation into a theorem. We regard the maintained separation, enforced by fail-closed machine-readable receipts, as a contribution of the program independent of any single result.

Boundary 1. This paper claims no completed derivation of particle masses, no source-only determination of the fine-structure constant, no completed cosmology, and no claim that formal verification establishes physical truth. Quantitative surfaces that consume measured values are labeled diagnostics and are never counted as predictions. Section 12 lists the decisive open gates.

None of the axioms contains a gauge group, a particle list, a spacetime dimension, or a recovery law. Everything below is an attempt to earn those structures, and the paper reports where the attempt succeeds, where it is conditional, and where it stops.

Descent, local diamonds, and a unique public record

Empirical meaning, falsifiability, and open obligations

The program maintains a preregistration architecture: custody-bound target definitions, kill bands, precision floors, and decision rules fixed before comparison, with falsifications retained at the same prominence as confirmations . Standing registered items include the conditional tau window above and a ringdown comb stance re-tested against each sufficiently loud gravitational-wave ringdown. The program’s empirical case rests on three decisive gates:

  1. Physical realization of the common refinement tower. The Einstein composition of Theorem 10 needs one source-derived tower satisfying its premises jointly; the signature instrument and its controls measure progress toward exactly this object.

  2. A source-only current and continuum producer. The gauge and matter results of Section 7 await laboratory current attachment and a continuum operator limit; identifiability audits delimit which attachments the registered source interface cannot supply.   3. One preregistered quantitative forecast not used in architecture selection. A sealed machine-readable prediction contract fixes the comparison custody. The first source-visible candidate is scored once; a complete negative inventory is an acceptable published outcome.

Each gate can fail, and the second one can fail provably. The program treats a rigorous non-identifiability theorem as a result of equal standing with a positive construction, because it tells the theory where additional observable structure must come from.

Discussion and comparison

The components of OPH have recognizable neighbors. Operational reconstructions of quantum theory derive Hilbert-space structure from information-theoretic postulates; OPH’s Section 4 differs by deriving the finite record calculus from a consensus fixed point rather than from axioms about state spaces. Algebraic and modular approaches to quantum gravity use Tomita–Takesaki flow as intrinsic time; OPH’s Einstein branch consumes modular structure the same way but insists on a finite, producer-typed route to the premises. Holographic and tensor-network programs relate bulk geometry to boundary entanglement; OPH’s screen is a boundary object throughout, and its capacity identities are finite-algebra statements at finite cutoff. Thermodynamic derivations of the Einstein equation from entropy stationarity are the closest relatives of Theorem 10; the program’s contribution there is the typed decomposition into separately certifiable premises with executable instruments. Distributed-computation views of physical consensus, quantum error correction, and relational interpretations each anticipate one ingredient; none, to our knowledge, combines finite observer consistency, confluent repair with formal certificates, measured signature instruments with mechanism controls, and an exact finite route to the Standard Model coefficient algebra and global form.

The novelty claim is therefore compositional. No single ingredient is unprecedented; the maintained chain from three observer axioms to the labeled structures above, under a verification and falsification discipline that a hostile auditor can execute, is the contribution offered for evaluation.

Two methodological positions are worth stating for a philosophy-of-physics readership. First, the program treats evidence-class separation as part of the theory’s content: a reconstruction whose conditional joints are hidden is a different, weaker object than the same mathematics with its joints displayed, and machine-enforced receipts require the display. Second, the program treats observer consistency as prior to state: A3 selects states from agreement constraints, reversing the usual order in which states are fundamental and intersubjective agreement is emergent. Whether that reversal ultimately earns the physics is exactly what the open gates of Section 12 will decide.
