What counts as a useful objection

A useful objection names a location and a failure mode. It can point to a wrong premise, invalid inference, unstated measured input, mismatch with standard physics, numerical mistake, reproducibility gap, or missing control in a public evidence bundle.

Quote the claim, link the source, identify the first failing step, and state what the step would require.

What does not help

General disbelief, unfamiliarity, or a claim's ambition can be a reason to inspect it closely. They do not identify a technical failure.

The review challenge asks a narrower question: where does an OPH claim first stop working?
