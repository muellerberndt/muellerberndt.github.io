Stress-test Observer Patch Holography.

OPH makes large claims. This page is an invitation to test them against the papers, code, simulations, and evidence.

A useful response names a broken derivation, hidden assumption, conflict with established physics, unsupported public claim, or empirical comparison that fails inspection. The local mathematical results, their physical interpretations, and the links between them have different status. One local equation has a checked solution. The proposed global capacity calculation gives no cosmic prediction because no general source argument or physical bridge is derived.

Send detailed objections, collaboration requests, and serious review notes to tom@floatingpragma.io.

Review target

Begin with the link from the finite mathematics to physical quantities. Check whether a premise is sufficient, an interpretation follows, a calculation can be reproduced, or a comparison was defined before the data was known. The global capacity proposal has no predictive status.

Computation boundary

The observer-screen computation essay concerns verification, mismatch detection, repair, and stable record formation. It does not claim to solve the classical P = NP problem.
