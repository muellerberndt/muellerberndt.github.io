I have been breaking software since the early 2000s. At SEC Consult in Vienna I found and exploited vulnerabilities in enterprise software, network appliances and phones; the Symbian research won the 2009 Pwnie Award for Most Innovative Research. In Singapore I started the OWASP Mobile Application Security Testing Guide and the Mobile Application Security Verification Standard. I wrote Mythril, a symbolic-execution security analyser for Ethereum smart contracts, and later built AI agents that audit code.

Since 2021 I have been working on repair networks: local parts that compare what they share and repair disagreement until the whole settles. The same idea drives my physics research, Observer Patch Holography, and Cadence, a brain that learns from live experience. Pragma Research is the independent lab for this work.
