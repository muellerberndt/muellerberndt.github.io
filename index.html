Research lines

Observer Patch Holography

OPH asks how limited observers can form one shared physical account. The mathematical work studies finite records, overlap consistency, repair, spacetime structure, and Standard Model Lie type. Exact results and physical identifications carry separate status.

Security and computation

Verifiable systems expose their boundary, state transitions, readback, and evidence. That same discipline informs work on zero-knowledge proofs, artificial intelligence security, and physical computation.
