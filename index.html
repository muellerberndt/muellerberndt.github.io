What we work on

Two research programs. One mathematics.

Our physics program asks whether the universe itself is built from observers that agree. Our machine learning program builds learning systems on the same principle and measures what they can do.

01 · Fundamental physics

Observer Patch Holography

A candidate unified theory that starts from observers instead of particles. Quantum probability, spacetime, gauge forces and the constants of nature are treated as consequences of one requirement: partial views of the world have to agree where they overlap.

  • One framework for quantum theory and gravity, derived from a consistency principle
  • More than 10,700 machine-checked theorems in Lean, with no admitted proofs
  • A classification theorem that singles out the Standard Model gauge structure
  • No axiom contains a gauge group, a particle list or a fitted constant

02 · Machine learning

Cadence

Neural networks that learn the way brains do. Every unit repairs its own state from its neighbours until the network comes to rest, and the resting state is the answer. Learning is the same process nudged toward a goal, with no backward pass.

  • One local rule for both answering and learning
  • Learns cart-pole from reward in under half the steps of PPO, with a fifth of the parameters
  • Scales from a six-unit toy to a 161,827-neuron nervous system read from a connectome
  • Open source under the MIT license

The common thread

Local agreement builds global structure.

The field at the top of this page runs the idea in miniature. Hundreds of oscillators, each coupled only to its neighbours, find a shared rhythm with no conductor. Disturb a region and the surrounding field pulls it back into line. Both programs run on the mathematics of distributed consensus, where agreement comes out of local comparison and repair with no central authority and no global view.

How we work

Every claim ships with its evidence.

Proof

Machine-checked mathematics

Core results are formalized in Lean 4 and checked by a proof kernel. A reader can verify them without trusting us.

Measurement

A receipt for every number

Each benchmark result binds the number to the exact code and data digests. Held-out data is read once, and baselines run in the same script.

Risk

Falsifiable by design

Physics predictions are frozen and time-stamped before the data arrives, with the results that would kill them written down in advance.

Access

Open by default

Papers, proofs, code and simulations are public. Cadence is MIT licensed, and every open physics problem is a GitHub issue.
