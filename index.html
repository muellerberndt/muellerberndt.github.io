The one result

Reality and thought run on the same process.

Our physics program proves that a stable, shared world emerges when bounded observers compare their records where their views overlap and repair the disagreement locally. That is the solution to physics. Our AGI program builds machines that do exactly that, and reads the state they settle into as a thought. That is the key to AGI. The field at the top of this page runs the idea in miniature: hundreds of oscillators, each coupled only to its neighbours, find one rhythm with no conductor.
