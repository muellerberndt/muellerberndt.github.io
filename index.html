The idea

Small models.
A shared answer.

Cadence connects local predictive patches. Each holds state and records, reads through its connections, and repairs prediction mismatches.

Flat settlement learns direct responses. State-coupled settlement coordinates populations. Recursive observers also read the brain's own prediction errors.
