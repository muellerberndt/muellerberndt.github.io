Inside the arcade

From watching
to playing.

Each brain is born when the page opens and learns while it plays. There is no training phase and no frozen copy: the brain you watch is the brain that learns.

It is a Cadence System 1 brain, modeled on an animal brain: a retina, an association cortex with a working memory, a motor cortex with one neuron per action, and a critic whose surprise acts like dopamine. Every action is read back from the settled state of the whole brain, and its outcome is kept as changed connections.

System 2, optional observers that read the brain's own activity, can be added to the same brain. This arcade runs System 1 alone.

Cadence 0.70 running in JavaScript, checked against the Python library. In recorded runs both brains took the controls within two minutes of birth and played at their teacher's level.
