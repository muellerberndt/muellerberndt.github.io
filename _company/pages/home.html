The ambition

A robot should learn from what happens to it. Every changed payload. Every unfamiliar surface. Every correction.

Our goal is a brain that carries experience forward: adapting to its body, recovering when conditions change and becoming more useful with practice.

Cadence gives us a different way to pursue that goal. Local predictive models observe one another, settle together and learn within a single coupled system.

A different architecture

Many local models.
One working brain.

Inspired by the cortex. Built from the mathematics of self-reading, cooperating observers.

01

Observe.

Bounded patches hold local state and records. Ports connect each patch to what it can read: its world, its neighbors and its own errors.

02

Settle.

Models exchange feedback and repair disagreement. Observer populations read activity and error within the same recursive settlement.

03

Learn.

Experience changes the network. Measurements and evidence bundles let us trace what changed and test whether behavior improved.
