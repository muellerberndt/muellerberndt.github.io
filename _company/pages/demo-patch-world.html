Inside the world

A brain has
to earn its keep.

A moving sun supplies energy while total mass stays fixed. Each creature pays for movement, brain patches and the work of settling them.

The simulation implements the Cadence 0.70 population solver in JavaScript and is checked against the library. Each brain is one connected settlement of state-coupled stages. Within a life, learning adjusts relations from action consequences, here in your browser. Across generations, mutation changes the inherited layout, including optional observer stages that read prediction errors.

Use the three views to move between the physical world, collective observation and an individual creature's beliefs. The published source includes deterministic probes and two 6,000-tick runs with mass conservation checks.
