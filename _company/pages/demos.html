Cadence / Demos

Brains that learn
while they run.

Watch brains learn live in your browser, evolve across generations and adapt to a body that changes. Every demo is working software from cadence-demos.

Cadence is real-time AI modeled after animal and human brains. These brains learn, remember and act while they run, with no training phase.

01 / Live learning in your browser

Atari Arcade.

Two brains born on page load.
Learning to play while you watch.

Each game runs on an Atari 2600 emulator in your browser. A newborn Cadence brain watches a teacher play, takes the controls on its own and keeps learning from the reward of its own actions. There is no training phase: the brain you watch is the brain that learns.

The brain is modeled on an animal's: a retina, an association cortex with a working memory, a motor cortex, and a critic whose surprise acts like dopamine.

In recorded runs both brains took the controls within two minutes of birth and played at their teacher's level.

02 / Learning and evolution in your browser

Patch World.

Brains that learn in one life
and evolve across generations.

A moving sun feeds a world with a fixed supply of mass. Every creature is born untrained and learns in your browser tab from the consequences of its own actions. Running a brain costs mass, so a bigger brain has to pay for itself.

Brain designs are inherited and mutate at each birth: size, depth and senses. Energy and death decide which designs survive. Select a creature to watch its brain at work.

Enter the world ↗Source and recorded runs ↗

Within a life

Experience changes the brain.

There is no training phase. Each creature acts, sees what followed and adjusts its own connections, for as long as it lives.

Across generations

Evolution changes the design.

Offspring inherit a mutated brain plan and start untrained. Each one has to learn its world for itself.

03 / For robotics teams / Runs locally

Rover Lab.

Change the body.
The live brain adapts.

A simulated rover learns how its wheel commands turn into motion, from executed commands and odometry alone. Weaken its right wheel without telling it. The learning brain notices within seconds of driving, relearns how its body moves and keeps reaching its targets.

A frozen copy of the same brain, driving the same weakened rover, falls behind.

Run Rover Lab ↗How it is built ↗Explore a robotics partnership ↗

One mode

No training phase.

The same brain predicts and learns on every control step. There is no switch between training and use.

Measured consequences

It learns from what the body did.

Only executed commands and measured motion teach the brain. Wheel strength, the phase of the experiment and the change itself are hidden from it.

Real time

It keeps a control deadline.

Every control step is timed against a 100 ms deadline, and any miss is reported. A whole life can be saved and resumed.

04 / Play in your browser

AMEN.

A small learned brain.
A room full of jungle.

Press CUT A DUB. AMEN composes a 16- or 32-bar track from what it has learned, then renders the drums and bass for playback. Adjust the variation and cut another.

The composer learned from about six hours of music in a little over an hour on a laptop. It keeps two kinds of memory, as brains do: slowly learned habits for the style and one-shot records of single moments.
