Inside the composer

Music from
learned records.

AMEN learned jungle from about six hours of music, in a little over an hour on a laptop.

It keeps two kinds of memory, as brains do: slowly learned habits carry the style, and one-shot records hold single moments. It starts from silence and hears every note it plays, so each next note depends on the last.

This brain learned beforehand. The page composes the track and renders its audio before playback.
