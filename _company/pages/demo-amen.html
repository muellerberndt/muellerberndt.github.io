Inside the composer

Music from
learned records.

AMEN learned jungle from 6.2 hours of music, in 78 CPU minutes on a laptop.

It keeps two kinds of memory, as brains do: slowly learned habits carry the style, and one-shot records hold single moments. It starts from silence and hears every note it plays, so each next note depends on the last.

A Cadence 0.70 record patch. The page composes the track and renders its audio before playback; the brain's parameters stay fixed in your browser.
