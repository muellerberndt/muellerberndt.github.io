03 / 08 · The technology

Modeled after animal and human brains.

Most of today's AI runs on transformers: completely feed-forward, and typically deployed with attention matrices and all other weights fixed once training ends. Cadence runs in real time and keeps learning from live experience.

It learns on the job

The same brain acts and learns from the result of every action. There is no training phase before deployment.

It adapts to change

A worn motor or a new load shows up in what the robot senses. The brain adjusts without being told what changed.

It does not act on a guess

Cadence acts only on a settled answer. When it has none, it reports that and the robot can fall back.

Cadence is built from small self-reading patches, each with its own state, connections, records and feedback. They settle on one answer together. How Cadence works
