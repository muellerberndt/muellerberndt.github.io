03 / 08 · The discovery

A theory of cooperating observers, made executable.

Bounded, self-reading patches hold local state, ports and records. They settle to answer and learn from experience. One settling brain, with observers as an option.

System 1, the default

A continuing brain with perception, plastic connections, memory and action. It learns from the outcome of every action, with no separate training mode.

System 2, optional

Observing regions read the brain's own activity and return feedback inside the same settlement. Observers can observe observers.

One settlement

Every answer is the settled state of the whole brain. A brain that does not settle refuses to act instead of guessing.

System 2 extends System 1 and shares its learning and qualification. Cortical organization supplies inspiration for the computational hypothesis. Architecture and research
