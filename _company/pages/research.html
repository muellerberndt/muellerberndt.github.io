Observer Patch Holography.

AI

Cadence.

Cadence applies the patch-net idea to learning machines. Its brain learns from experience, as animal brains do and not by backpropagation: System 1 by default, with optional System 2 observers. The preprint describes the architecture and the computational hypothesis behind learning through settlement.
