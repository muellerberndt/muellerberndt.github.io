01 / Physics

Observer Patch Holography.

Bounded, self-reading patches hold local state and records, compare what they share through their boundaries, and repair disagreements. OPH develops the physical structures supported by that observer architecture.

Explore the physics ↗

02 / Mathematics

Make the argument checkable.

Finite consensus, normal forms and event algebras turn the observer idea into explicit constructions. Lean formalizations check stated implications; executable models connect the equations to reproducible experiments.

Read the foundations ↓

03 / AI

Cadence.

A deep real-time brain that learns from experience. Flat layouts read sensors, ordinary state-coupled layouts add intermediate representations, and recursive observers also read exact prediction errors. All three share the same patch, repair and qualification mechanism.
