The opportunity

We build the brains. Robot manufacturers build the bodies.

Robotics is our first market. The proposed product is a supported Cadence runtime and a brain prepared for the customer's embodiment, with a path from one useful integration to recurring fleet revenue.

A changed payload, actuator response or work surface can turn a familiar action into a failed one. Our first customer test is whether retained experience reduces repeated intervention after such a change.

01

Paid evaluation

Integrate one valuable task with a robot maker. Measure adaptation against its existing stack and agreed resource budgets.

02

Production license

Convert useful results into a runtime and embodiment license, with deployment integration and support.

03

Fleet expansion

Expand through additional robots and tasks using the same brain architecture and reusable integration tools.
