## The big questions

### Why is there something rather than nothing?

The core intuition behind OPH is that reality is the self-answering question: “What would a universe that creates itself consistently be like?” The key word is consistently: mathematical and logical consistency, free of contradiction. "Something outside made it" needs an outside and a before, the very things in question. "It is just here" explains nothing. Nothing at all fails too, since an empty world holds no observer and no record, so no truth about it is held anywhere. Our universe is the consistent answer. Inside it, the world evolves through local disagreement and repair: neighbors compare records, repair what disagrees, and what survives every comparison is fact. Its observers reverse-engineer the rules behind their records and build the process that realizes this same history. Reaching that event is a mathematically guaranteed fixed point, and it exists in our subjective future just as our past exists. Nothing outside the history is needed to start it.

### What does it mean to call the universe a fixed-point solver?

A fixed point is an object returned unchanged by a specified map: applying F to x gives x again. A solver searches for an object satisfying that condition. OPH's closure proposal asks for a history whose internally recovered description, when realized, reproduces the same observations and interventions. A Sudoku is an accessible analogy: the finished grid simultaneously satisfies the row, column, box and clue constraints. A solver's sequence of guesses is different from the grid's defining relations. Likewise, no external clock has to run a universe-wide search before time begins. Different maps can have no fixed point, one or many. Writing the equation supplies a constraint; constructing the inhabited physical solution supplies the substantive work.

### How does OPH answer Zeno’s paradox?

Zeno asks how a runner can finish a journey if crossing it requires first crossing half, then half the remainder, forever. The mathematical response is that infinitely many shrinking intervals can have a finite total: one half plus one quarter plus one eighth and so on sums to one. OPH adds a physical interpretation. ## Time

### What is time?

Time is three different things sharing one word: order, direction and flow. Order is which event depended on which. A later repair certifies that it read a value an earlier one wrote, and pairs with no such chain between them have no fact about which came first. Direction comes from the repair itself, which can average seven possible pasts of a seam into one reading and destroy the difference. Flow is what a region's own state makes its questions do, a reversible motion computed from the odds it holds, with its rate built in. A clock joins them, as an observer that counts every repair touching it and turns the count into phase. Tallies, waves, light signals and a region's own flow all read one parameter, because each is a reading of the same repair history.

### Why does time only go forward?

Time goes one way because every repair throws information away, and nothing can put it back. When two neighboring readings disagree, both move to their midpoint. A seam holding 6 and 0 ends at 3 and 3, and so do 5 and 1, 4 and 2, and four other starting pairs. Seven arrangements go in, one comes out, and about 2.8 bits about the past are gone. The loss is arithmetic, so it needs no special low-entropy beginning for the universe and holds on the first repair as on the ten billionth. The arrow is a ratio. A step producing ten bits of entropy runs forward about a thousand times as often as backward, so a bead in water watched for a hundredth of a second can be caught running backwards, and a cup of coffee never is.

### Is there a universal "now"?

There is no universal present moment, since no instrument reads one and no procedure produces one. ### Why does time feel like it flows?

OPH traces the feeling to the part of you that is not made of records. Every observer holds a set of questions about its own patch of the world and a state, the odds it puts on each answer. ### Why do all clocks agree?

All clocks agree because each one is a reading of the same repair history. The basic clock counts every repair that touches a region, idle ones included. Regions are touched at different rates, yet each count, divided by its expected rate, converges on one parameter, with nobody synchronizing anything. The fluctuations of that traffic form a field that relaxes in the same count. A small memory stepped once per touch oscillates as a wave in that count. A disturbance reaches a distant region exactly when the count equals their distance, which makes the speed of light the exchange rate between count and ruler. A region's own internal flow matches too, scaled by its temperature. Satellite clocks gain forty-five microseconds a day because they sit where the settled state is colder, and they agree after conversion. Attaching normalized count ratios directly to published optical clock frequency ratios is rejected by the data, so a count becomes a reading in seconds only through a calibration that models the measuring apparatus.

### Why do moving clocks run slow?

A moving clock runs slow because each tick has further to travel at a speed that never changes. Take a clock of two mirrors one meter apart with a pulse of light bouncing between them, riding on a train. Seen from the platform, the mirrors slide sideways while the pulse is in flight, so the pulse runs along a slant, which is longer than the gap. Light has one speed for everybody, so the longer path takes longer and the tick stretches. By Pythagoras, at eighty percent of light speed a tick lasts five thirds as long. Cosmic-ray muons should decay long before reaching the ground, and they arrive anyway, on stretched ticks. Underneath the mirrors, a duration is a count of records in small regions strung along a path, and a moving path collects fewer of them.

### Is the past real?

The past is real, and nothing outside the world deletes it. Its content is fixed, because rewriting it would need information the commits destroyed. The everyday picture has a present sweeping forward and leaving the past behind. That sweep would need a clock outside the history to advance it, and there is none, so nothing outside erases what lies behind. Inside the history, events are ordered wherever dependencies connect them, records form and local states change. What happens to a particular record is a matter for the dynamics inside. ## The quantum world

### Why is the world quantum?

The world is quantum because the questions an observer can put to it do not all commute, so asking one and then another differs from the reverse order. That refusal is what ties observers together. If every question could be answered alongside every other, each observer's answers would be an independent row in a giant spreadsheet, and nothing one observer held would constrain what another holds. Questions that commute with everything collect in one corner, called the center, and that is where records live. That corner looks exactly like classical physics, a world of tables and pointer readings. A record layer like that cannot change smoothly and reversibly, so whatever flows in the world flows in the part that refuses to commute. With the questions fixed, the rule for odds has one possible form, the Born rule.

### Why can't you know position and momentum at the same time?

You can't know both because position and momentum are two questions with no joint answer. Socks then shoes is a different operation from shoes then socks, and asking position then momentum likewise differs from asking momentum then position. The difference between the two orders, called the commutator, is a fixed amount with Planck's constant in it. No apparatus appears in that statement, so no better apparatus shrinks it. The uncertainty relation reads that amount as a floor under the spread in position times the spread in momentum. Squeeze an electron into an atom-sized region, a tenth of a nanometer across, and the spread in its speed cannot drop below about six hundred kilometers a second. There is no state of the electron with a sharp position and a sharp speed, so the measurement has nothing to disturb.

### What is quantum entanglement?

Entanglement is definiteness that lives in the relation between two systems and in neither one alone. ### Why do quantum correlations beat classical physics, but only by 2√2?

Quantum correlations beat classical ones because each side's two questions refuse to be answered together, and they stop at 2√2 because that refusal has a maximum size. ### What is a particle?

A particle is a knot in the way neighboring patches of the world translate each other's readings, a twist that no local repair can undo. ### What is the wavefunction?

The wavefunction is a way of writing down what an observer can ask and the odds of each answer. Different observers with different lists of questions build different spaces, and they agree wherever their lists overlap. That overlap is the only sense in which the wavefunction is public. ### What is quantum superposition?

### What is the Born rule?

## Energy, heat and information

### What is energy?

Energy is the rate at which a region's records are worked through by repair, counted on that region's own clock. The world has one move. ### Is mathematics invented or discovered?

### What is symmetry in physics?

A symmetry is a change in how the world is written down that leaves a named quantity alone, which makes it a claim that part of the description was never physical. Longitude shows it. Latitude has a zero the planet supplies, the equator. Longitude has none, because turning the whole Earth about its axis changes no measurable relation between places. So in October 1884 delegates from twenty-six nations met in Washington and voted for a zero, and Greenwich won twenty-two to one. Distances came through the vote unchanged. Physics runs the same operation on bigger descriptions. The laws are the same here and elsewhere, today and in the Cretaceous, and Emmy Noether proved in 1918 that those two symmetries give conservation of momentum and of energy. Scale is no symmetry. Atoms have a definite size.

### What is momentum?

### Why do symmetries give conservation laws?

Every continuous symmetry hands back a conserved quantity, by a fixed recipe Emmy Noether published in 1918. A symmetry is a change that leaves the laws alone. Being fitter today predicts nothing about the day after, because the flask a lineage competes in is the flask it is changing. OPH adds one condition at the scale of the whole universe. A universe that reads itself into existence needs intelligence, so its history has to contain an evolution that reaches minds. That is a requirement on the whole, and no single step of selection aims at it.

### Are we morally responsible for what we do?

Yes. Responsibility rests on the will that produced an act and on the record of the act, and it needs no proof that some other act was possible. Harry Frankfurt showed this in 1969 with a thought experiment. Black wants Jones to do a certain thing and stands ready to force him if Jones shows any sign of choosing otherwise. Jones does it on his own, and Black never moves. Jones could not have done otherwise, and he is responsible anyway, because the act came out of his own deliberation. Whether some other setting of the world was reachable is a question about the space of possible records. Which entry got written, out of whose repair policy, is a question about this record, and this record is what everything downstream is computed from.

### Is it better to tell a painful truth or keep the peace?

Tell it, when writing it down lowers what everyone involved will have to repair later. An act is scored by what it does to the repair work of everyone it reaches, added up over a horizon long enough to hold its consequences. Comfort next week sits inside that sum with a weight, and so does everything after next week, so a move that raises the load for a month can lower the total. A household that keeps its peace by leaving something unsaid holds its own mismatch flat, and the people who inherit the unwritten record pay for the arrangement. Niceness that preserves abuse blocks repair and transfers the cost forward. A difficult confession costs the most, because the true thing it puts on the shared record is about the one confessing.

### Are animals conscious?

Many are, and the science supports it. An animal that can suffer is a moral patient, and its suffering counts whenever anyone's actions reach it.

### What makes humans different from other animals?

### If intelligence matters to the universe, where is everybody? (The Fermi paradox)

The Fermi paradox contrasts the apparent opportunities for extraterrestrial life with the lack of an agreed detection of technological civilizations. OPH's proposal that intelligent observers participate in their world's reconstruction does not predict how many civilizations exist, how long they last or whether they communicate with us. A role for observers is not a forecast of visible spacecraft. Distances, timing, biology and detectability must be modeled rather than inferred from the closure idea. The thesis therefore gives a reason to investigate intelligence as part of the physical history, while leaving the astronomical population question to evidence. Without a quantitative population and signal model, OPH has no established solution to the apparent silence.

## About OPH

### What is OPH?

OPH is a rigorous, consistent and complete description of the unified, self-creating computational Universe we inhabit. The letters stand for Observer Patch Holography. It starts with observers, bounded systems that read their surroundings, keep records and settle disagreements with their neighbors, and it assumes no space, time, fields or measured constants. Where two observers overlap, their records must agree. Objective reality is what their shared records read out once disagreements are repaired. Whatever persists under that repair is physics: conserved totals, three directions of space, clocks that agree, fields, and particles, which are knots no repair can undo. Holography names the fact that all anyone learns about a region arrives through its surface. The lowest layer is one fixed rule of repair and memory, and it runs on a laptop. Nothing outside runs the world. It computes itself, and its observers close the loop by recovering its law from their records and building the screen that computes them.

### Is OPH a religion?

No. OPH began as an attempt to reconstruct physics from observers with no religious premise, and its physical claims come with measurements that would refute them. If dark energy's pressure-to-density ratio is ever measured to change over cosmic time, the fixed record capacity of the universe falls, and the derivation of the fine-structure constant goes with it. A single proton decaying through a unified-force channel takes down the argument about the proton. The central calculation on twelve observers covers 2,048 states and can be done by hand in an evening. The account arrives at ideas that religions named long ago, among them judgment, resurrection, paradise and hell, and reads their traditions as partial dictionaries for one structure of records, repair and continuation, none holy by its label alone. Restoration in OPH is work that future observers will most likely do with technology, inside the world, and nobody is asked to accept it on authority.

### Isn't a self-creating universe circular?

No, because the loop is an equation, and circularity is a fault of arguments. A circular argument assumes its conclusion in order to prove it. An equation such as x = 1 + 1/x has the unknown on both sides and picks out one positive number, 1.6180339887, which a calculator reaches from any positive start. The universe's loop works the same way. It is a condition on a description and the world it describes, taken together, and a history either satisfies it or fails. A loop that let every history through would explain nothing, and this one rules out every history whose observers could not recover its rules and build the screen that computes it. Nothing in it has to come first. Part of the condition can be tested on hardware. Split the computation across a thousand machines and it finishes in the state a single machine reaches.

### Isn't this just what Leibniz said?

Leibniz had the objection, and OPH supplies the arithmetic he lacked. Writing to Samuel Clarke until his death in 1716, he argued that space is only the order of things that coexist. Shift the universe three feet to the left and nothing measurable changes, so absolute position is a fact about a picture. Berkeley restated it in 1721 and Mach in 1883. Leibniz was right, and absolute space stayed in the textbooks for two centuries, because an objection that forbids a quantity without predicting a measurement changes nothing. Here the objection arrives with a wiring diagram, twelve ports and thirty seams per piece of the world, one averaging rule forced by three demands, and forces split as one plus three plus eight. Leibniz had no wiring diagram, which is why Clarke could answer him through five letters and concede nothing.
