PRIVACY

This site is run by Bernhard Mueller (Pragma Research). Questions about your data go to info@floatingpragma.ai.

Measurement. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and Google Ads to measure which ads lead to visits and messages. Both set cookies and send Google your browser details, the pages you open and an approximate location. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland these tags stay off until you accept them. Google's own terms describe how it handles the data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Forms. The join and investors forms send your message by email to Bernhard Mueller through the email service Resend. The site keeps no copy and there is no mailing list. Your email address is used only to reply to you.

Your choice. You can change your cookie choice at any time: cookie settings. To have a message deleted from the inbox, write to the address above.
