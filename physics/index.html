The claim

The universe is a computation. We found the machine.

Simulation theory is usually a thought experiment: perhaps the world is a program. Observer Patch Holography turns it into a mathematical result. It asks what the smallest system capable of having a world at all must look like, and then proves how much of known physics follows from that system alone.

The answer has three layers. The hardware is a patch net of bounded observers, each with a twelve-port screen. The software is the protocol by which patches record what they see, compare records where their views overlap and repair the disagreement. The output is the public world every observer inside it shares. Quantum probability, thermodynamics, 3+1 spacetime, the Standard Model gauge structure and fixed-point equations for the constants come out of that loop. No gauge group, particle list or fitted constant goes in. There is no programmer outside the machine: the universe is the fixed point of its own readback and repair.

The machine

Hardware, software, output.

01 · Hardware

Twelve-port observer patches

Every observer is a bounded system with local state, a boundary, memory and a way to read its neighbours. Its screen has twelve ports wired as the corners of an icosahedron: degree five with spherical closure forces exactly 12 vertices, 30 edges and 20 faces. Patches join through typed seams into one net.

  • No patch sees the whole universe
  • All 1,352,078 states of the screen were checked: the repair law leaves exactly one resting state
02 · Software

Record, compare, repair, agree

Each patch keeps records of what it sees. Where two patches overlap, their records are compared. Disagreements are repaired by local moves only. A consensus theorem guarantees that every repair order ends in the same public record, and whatever agreement leaves open is as random as possible.

  • Zero adjustable numbers anywhere in the axioms
  • A distributed consensus protocol with no central authority and no global clock
03 · Output

The universe you observe

The public record the patches settle into is the objective world. Its structure is forced: finite event algebras carry the Born rule, repair carries the four laws of thermodynamics, records between events carry time, the screen's reversible response carries the forces.

  • 3+1 spacetime and a route to the Einstein field equation
  • su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1), three colours, anomaly-free matter

The breaking point

Detune the observers and the world dissolves.

Each patch runs at its own natural rhythm. Repair absorbs a small spread and the patches lock into one account that all of them hold. Widen the spread far enough and repair loses. The patches drift apart, the shared rhythm goes, and no public record forms at all.

Drag the handle to set how far apart the rhythms are. The wheel carries the phase of every patch, the arrow is how much they agree, and the tiles are the patches themselves.

Distributed systems hit the same wall when clocks drift faster than the protocol reconciles them. Physics inherits the consequence: a set of observers shares a world only while repair keeps up with how much they differ.

The axioms

Three axioms. Zero dials.

OPH starts from three assumptions and no adjustable numbers. No axiom contains a gauge group, a particle list or a fitted constant. Everything else has to be earned.

  • The screen. Every observer has a twelve-port icosahedral screen. Degree five with spherical closure forces exactly 12 vertices, 30 edges and 20 faces. A cube cannot do it.
  • Agreement. Observers whose views overlap agree on shared meaning.
  • Maximum randomness. Whatever the first two axioms leave open is as random as possible.

Falsifiable

How to kill this theory.

A theory that cannot lose cannot win. OPH freezes its predictions before the data arrives, and the first frozen set is time-stamped on the Bitcoin blockchain. Any of these observations would break it.

  • A fourth light generation of chiral matter.
  • A number of quark colours other than three.
  • An additional elementary Higgs boson.
  • A tau lepton mass measured outside 1776.968991 to 1776.969063 MeV, the window fixed by the theory's lepton balance premise.
What it opens

Every unification became technology.

Maxwell's unification gave us radio. Quantum mechanics gave us the transistor and the laser. Relativity keeps satellite navigation accurate. A theory that says physical law is a repair computation gives an engineering rule nobody had before: any medium that relaxes to a fixed point is a computer, and the repair equations are its design spec.
