Observer Patch Holography

The research behind the brain.

Could a shared physical world emerge from bounded observers that compare their records and repair disagreement? Observer Patch Holography explores that question through mathematics, machine-checked proofs and executable models. Its organizing idea also inspired Cadence.

Explore the papers Watch the research explainer

The central idea

A world built from what observers can share.

An observer is a bounded, self-reading system. It has local state, ports or boundaries, records, and feedback that lets it repair disagreement.

Each patch has access to part of a larger system. Where patches overlap, their records must agree on shared meaning. OPH studies the structures that follow when those local relationships support a consistent public account.

The research connects record agreement with quantum probability, geometry, the symmetry pattern of the known forces and conditional gravitational dynamics. The papers state the assumptions each connection requires.

The mathematical case

One architecture connects several parts of physics.

The strongest case for OPH is the range of explicit constructions that fit within its observer architecture.

Records and probability

Agreement with mathematical structure.

Finite normal-form theorems describe agreement, stability and refinement. On the stated event-algebra branch, the record structure supports quantum probabilities, the Tsirelson bound and no-cloning.

Quantum records

Geometry and forces

From local response to familiar symmetries.

A twelve-port construction supplies a three-dimensional response space. Under its complete-response and transport hypotheses, a classification gives the Standard Model gauge Lie algebra. A declared matter construction gives an anomaly-free fifteen-state generation.

Gauge and matter structure

Spacetime and gravity

Explicit routes from records to dynamics.

Declared source and reading laws support controlled spacetime constructions. A common physical realization with the stated stress, entropy, continuum and scale data yields an Einstein-equation implication up to a cosmological constant.

Spacetime reconstruction

What the evidence establishes.

Formal proofs establish consequences of specified assumptions. Reproducible simulations test the declared models. Establishing that this architecture describes nature requires physical realization and measurements that distinguish its predictions. Numerical matches that use measured targets are diagnostics.

From physics to intelligence

Make the organizing idea executable.

Our hypothesis is that intelligence can arise from local predictive models that settle and learn from experience.

Cadence brings the OPH idea of self-reading, repairing patches together with the cortical idea of cooperating local models. Flat input-only, ordinary state-coupled and recursive observer layouts all use the same repair and qualification procedure. Recursive observers add access to other populations' activity and exact prediction errors within the shared settlement.

This is an implemented computational hypothesis. Cortical organization supplies inspiration; the software is evaluated through learning behavior, adaptation and measured resource use. The commercial case rests on what a Cadence brain can do inside a body.
